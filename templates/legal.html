Information we store

CalFit stores account details (name and email), a securely hashed password, exercises you add, and workout entries you choose to save. The starter application uses a local SQLite database.

How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, manage your exercise library, generate circuits and display your workout history. This starter build does not connect to wearable devices or sell personal information.

Security and retention

Passwords are stored as hashes. For a public deployment, configure a strong secret key, HTTPS, backups, access controls and a documented data-retention process. You should add account deletion and export workflows before collecting real user data.
